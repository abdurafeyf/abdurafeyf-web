Hi there! I'm Rafey, a first-year PhD student in **Biomedical Engineering** at [Purdue University](https://purdue.edu), where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant under the supervision of [Dr. Rachel K. Surowiec](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rachel-surowiec/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **medical image analysis, multimodal representation learning, and inverse problems in biomedical imaging**. Currently, my work focuses on quantitative bone imaging, particularly extracting meaningful information from clinical CT scans to better understand skeletal health. I'm interested in developing computational methods for image reconstruction, segmentation, and quantitative analysis, with an emphasis on making medical imaging more accessible and clinically useful.

Before joining Purdue, I completed my undergraduate degree in **Software Engineering** at the [National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST)](https://nust.edu.pk), Pakistan. My undergraduate thesis explored the interpretability of medical images for generating coherent radiology reports, under the supervision of [Dr. Hashir M. Kiani](https://seecs.nust.edu.pk/faculty/hashir-moheed-kiani/).

I was also part of the [Machine Vision & Intelligent Systems Lab (MachVis)](https://vision.seecs.edu.pk/), working with [Dr. Nazia Perwaiz](https://seecs.nust.edu.pk/faculty/nazia-perwaiz/). Our research focused on generating pathology reports from gigapixel histopathological images while investigating generalizability across diverse ethnic and regional datasets. This work was associated with [MICCAI '25](https://reg2025.grand-challenge.org/reg2025/).

Previously, I was a [Mitacs Globalink](https://globalink.mitacs.ca/) Research Intern at the **University of Saskatchewan**, where I worked with [Dr. Zadia Codabux](https://www.cs.usask.ca/faculty/zadiacodabux/research.html) to investigate the impact of Technical Debt (TD) on open-source software projects using natural language processing techniques.

I also spent an exchange semester at [Utah Tech University](https://utahtech.edu/), studying Distributed Computing, Machine Learning, and American Government, while finding time for piano and pickleball.
